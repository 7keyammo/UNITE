OBSIDIAN LONG-TERM MEMORY—
DaQauntum writes its memory into the vault as linked notes, and indexes your own notes back as citable sources.

The vault is a projection: DaQauntum's database stays authoritative and the vault can be regenerated. Your notes become evidence DaQauntum can cite — retrieval is not proof, so nothing there becomes a fact on its own. DaQauntum never re-indexes its own exported notes, so it cannot cite itself as corroboration.
